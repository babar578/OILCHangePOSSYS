## 🎯 **Customization Options**

### Change Logo Icon

Current: `<i class="fas fa-gem"></i>` (Diamond/Gem icon)

To match bull head, you can use:
- `<i class="fas fa-dragon"></i>` - Dragon (powerful)
- `<i class="fas fa-shield-alt"></i>` - Shield (security)
- Or upload your actual logo image

**To use image logo**:
```html
<div class="bull-icon">
    <img src="/Content/images/torrosell-logo.png" alt="TorroSell" style="width:100%;height:100%;object-fit:contain;" />
</div>
```

### Change Accent Color

Find and replace `#6272a4` with your preferred color:
- Brighter blue: `#4a90e2`
- Purple: `#9b59b6`
- Green: `#27ae60`

### Change Background Darkness

Adjust these values:
- Lighter: `#40444b`
- Darker: `#1e2124`
